IPAF

Mobile Elevating Work Platforms

IPAF certification for the operation of mobile elevating work platforms (MEWPs) and telescopic handlers. Our operators are qualified for all categories required by regulations.

  • Categories 1A, 1B, 3A, 3B qualification
  • Vertical MEWPs, boom-type and truck-mounted
  • Biennial renewal with practical refresher

CAT A — SUVA crane operator licence

Tower crane and mobile crane qualification

Category A and B crane operator licence issued by SUVA for operating tower cranes, mobile cranes and truck-mounted cranes on site, with continuous training.

  • Rotating and non-rotating tower cranes
  • Certified theoretical and practical training
  • Mandatory periodic refresher

Work at height

Fall protection and harnesses

Specific training for work at height using Category III PPE: harnesses, lifelines, anchor points and fall arrest systems compliant with SUVA regulations.

  • Correct use of harnesses and lanyards
  • Restraint and positioning systems
  • Emergency and rescue procedures

Welding courses

Aluminium and steel welding

Training and certification for welding operators according to European standards. Practical courses for TIG, MIG/MAG welding on aluminium and steel for facade applications.

  • TIG and MIG/MAG welding
  • Aluminium, stainless steel and carbon steel
  • Certification according to EN ISO 9606

SUVA safety course

Accident prevention on site

Comprehensive SUVA safety course for accident prevention on site, risk management, correct use of PPE and emergency procedures.

  • Accident prevention and risk management
  • Correct use of PPE across all categories
  • First aid and emergency procedures
