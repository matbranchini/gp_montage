2. Personal data and processing of personal data

Personal data we process about you includes:

  • contact information such as name, postal address, email address or telephone number;
  • your position or title, as well as other basic information;
  • records relating to your visits to the website and your use of its features;
  • any other information relating to you that you may provide to us for the purpose of organising and planning our activities.

“Processing” means any operation performed on your personal data, such as:

  • collection and storage;
  • updating and modification;
  • use and application;
  • linking with other data;
  • transmission;
  • deletion and destruction of data.

We collect this information when you interact with us, when you communicate with our staff, visit our website or send us a request via the contact form.

3. For what purposes do we process personal data?

  • to communicate with you;
  • for archiving and other administrative purposes;
  • to analyse your requests and needs in order to provide services related to our business;
  • to administer and manage our IT resources and other resources.

4. To whom do we disclose your personal data?

Personal data is treated confidentially. Our employees have access to your personal data to the extent necessary for the purposes described and for their work with us. They are obliged to maintain confidentiality when processing personal data.

We may also disclose your personal data to third-party service providers whose services we use, such as our website hosting provider. They process data exclusively on our instructions and are contractually bound to implement appropriate security measures.

5. How do we process personal data in relation to the website?

When you visit our website, we process your personal data to provide the website and ensure IT security. The site uses only essential technical cookies, necessary for the proper functioning of the pages. No profiling or marketing cookies are used.

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores, for a limited period, log data when you access the website. This information includes: IP address, dates and times of access, pages visited, browser used and similar information. We use this data to manage, maintain, secure and improve our website.

5.1 Third-party cookies

Our website displays typefaces using Google Fonts. In order to display this site correctly, Google may receive personal information about you, including: browser, network or device information; information about the page being viewed; the IP address. The service is provided by Google LLC (USA) in accordance with its privacy policy.

5.2 Contact form

The contact form on this website does not send data to any external server or third-party service. When submitted, the browser opens the user’s default email client (e.g. Outlook, Mail, Gmail) with the message pre-filled. The actual transmission of data therefore takes place via the sender’s email provider, which is subject to its own privacy policy.

6. How long do we retain your personal data?

We retain your personal data for as long as is necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected and for as long as we have a legitimate interest in retaining it. We also retain your personal data for as long as it is subject to a statutory retention obligation.

7. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, use, loss, alteration or destruction. Our website uses SSL encryption.

8. When do we transfer your personal data abroad?

Recipients of your personal data may also be located abroad, such as outside the European Union or the European Economic Area. Those countries may not have laws protecting your personal data to the same extent as Switzerland, the EU or the EEA. When we transfer personal data to such countries, we take the necessary measures to protect it.

9. What rights do you have in relation to the processing of your personal data?

You may object to the processing of your data at any time. Within the legal framework applicable to you, you also have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to object to our processing and to receive your personal data in a readable format free of charge. You also have the right to withdraw your consent without this affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, write to: info@gpmontage.ch
You may also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
